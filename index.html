About

Hydrology that people can read and use.

I work across the whole chain: rainfall and river data, groundwater and monitoring records, GIS and remote sensing, numerical and statistical modelling, then the writing and visuals that carry the result to managers, donors and decision-makers.

Hydrologic and hydraulic modelling

From rainfall to flood maps

I build HEC-HMS catchment models that turn rainfall into runoff and flood hydrographs, then take those flows into HEC-RAS for river and floodplain hydraulics. GIS connects the whole chain, from terrain and land use to the final maps.

  1. Catchment setupTerrain, land use and soils prepared in GIS to define sub-basins.
  2. HEC-HMSRainfall–runoff and channel routing for design or event hydrographs.
  3. HEC-RASA 1D channel or 2D floodplain model, calibrated to the data available.
  4. Flood mappingDepth, extent and velocity maps, with a report on assumptions and sensitivity.
